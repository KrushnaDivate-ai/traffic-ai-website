PROJECT OVERVIEW

Probabilistic Reasoning System
for Traffic Prediction

A smart-city project that demonstrates how probabilistic reasoning can support traffic management and forecasting.

OBJECTIVE

Make uncertainty useful.

The goal is to predict traffic conditions even when the available information is incomplete or changing. Instead of returning only one rigid answer, the system provides probabilities and a confidence-aware result.
